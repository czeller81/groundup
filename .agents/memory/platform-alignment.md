---
name: Platform alignment guardrails
description: Durable product and trust constraints for Ground Up's two-path platform.
---

Ground Up keeps physical Training and Adaptive Capacity as separate product paths under one human-resilience brand; Adaptive Capacity must remain cohort-ready without invented dates, pricing, outcomes, or credentials.

**Why:** The platform is intentionally broader than a gym, but overpromising an unconfirmed cohort would undermine trust and create operational risk.

**How to apply:** Keep training leads and Adaptive Capacity interest-list leads distinguishable, and state unconfirmed program details as unconfirmed.
