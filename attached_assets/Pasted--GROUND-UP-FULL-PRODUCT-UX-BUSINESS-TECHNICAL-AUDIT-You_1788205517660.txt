# IMPORTANT AUDIT RULES

1. Do not assume functionality based only on source-code presence.
2. Test behavior where safe.
3. Distinguish clearly between:

   * verified working behavior
   * inferred behavior
   * configured but unverified behavior
   * missing behavior
4. Do not hide failures.
5. Do not quietly fix things during the audit.
6. Do not rewrite the application.
7. Do not publish.
8. Do not expose secrets or personal data.
9. Do not fabricate business facts or instructor credentials.
10. Be extremely critical of anything that could damage user trust.
11. Prioritize business viability over impressive technical complexity.
12. A simple working booking and lead funnel is more valuable right now than sophisticated features that customers do not need.
13. Preserve historical evidence of the current implementation so we have a true baseline before remediation.

The final deliverable should be a comprehensive audit report that I can use to decide exactly what Replit should fix next.
