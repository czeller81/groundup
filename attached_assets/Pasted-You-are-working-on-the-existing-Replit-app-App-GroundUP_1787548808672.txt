# 7. COHORT STRUCTURE

The 12-week journey has three phases.

## PHASE 1 — MINDSET & COLLABORATION

### Weeks 1–5

Focus:

* reframing problems
* understanding AI limits
* recognizing assumptions
* distinguishing fluent output from correct output
* learning how to validate
* building epistemic discipline

Core idea:

**The Validation Tether**

Participants must learn enough independently to:

* question
* verify
* modify
* reject
* override

AI output when needed.

Do not teach blind tool dependence.

---

## PHASE 2 — BUILD & REDESIGN

### Weeks 6–9

Focus:

* applying adaptive capacity directly to real jobs and workflows
* designing practical AI-supported systems
* testing assumptions
* preserving human judgment
* turning abstract insight into functional execution

Participants should create:

**An AI-augmented professional workflow built around a real problem.**

Use the VIBE sequence when appropriate:

* Vision
* Intelligence
* Business Logic
* Execution

Do not present this as generic prompt engineering.

---

## PHASE 3 — STEWARD & DEMONSTRATE

### Weeks 10–12

Focus:

* communicating value
* transferring capability to the real world
* labor-market relevance
* peer teaching
* reflection
* capstone demonstration

Participants should complete a capstone that may include:

* software
* service design
* workflow redesign
* operational improvement
* professional artifact

The capstone should demonstrate:

* independent understanding
* deliberate AI orchestration
* validation
* transferability
* value creation

# 8. ADAPTIVE PRACTICE LOOP

Introduce the idea that learning happens through repeated practice.

Use this general loop:

1. Attempt
2. Augment
3. Interrogate
4. Justify
5. Transfer
